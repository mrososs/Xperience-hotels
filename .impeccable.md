# Xperience Hotels — Project Design Guidelines

## Design Context

### Users
Public marketing & direct-booking site for **Xperience Hospitality Management** — six 5-star Red Sea resorts in Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt (member of Kiroseiz Group, 30+ years). It serves a broad leisure audience, all of whom matter equally:

- **Families with children** — aqua parks, family suites, kids' facilities.
- **Couples & honeymooners** — romance, privacy, spa, candle-lit dining ("for two" packages).
- **International leisure (Europe)** — sun-seeking holiday bookers, browsing in English.
- **GCC, regional & domestic travellers** — Arabic-first; the RTL experience is first-class, not a translation afterthought.

The job to be done: research resorts, feel confident in the brand, and **book direct** (VIP perks, spa/diving/dining discounts). Visitors are usually planning a holiday in a relaxed, aspirational headspace — often on mobile.

### Brand Personality
- **Three words:** Refined · Warm · Trustworthy.
- **Promise:** "International standards, regional warmth."
- **Emotional goal:** evoke **refined prestige** — quiet luxury and trust that signals a serious, established 5-star brand, while still feeling hospitable and human (never cold or stuffy).
- **Voice:** confident, gracious, unhurried. Bilingual EN/AR with full parity.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Elevate the existing luxury-minimal system** — do not reinvent it. Sharpen typography, spacing, motion and micro-detail; let whitespace and restraint do the work.

- **Palette:** warm cream canvas (`--xp-cream #f7f2ea`), refined ink neutrals, **champagne gold** (`--xp-gold #c0a36b`) as the sparing accent, Red Sea teal/deep-sea (`--xp-sea`) as secondary. Neutrals are warm-tinted, never pure black/white.
- **Type:** Playfair Display (display serif) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body); **El Messiri** for Arabic. Fluid modular scale (`clamp`), strong size/weight contrast.
- **Theme:** light, airy, editorial. Imagery (Red Sea, resorts) is the colour; the UI stays neutral.
- **Motion:** subtle scroll reveals, gentle ken-burns hero, count-ups — restraint over spectacle. One well-orchestrated moment beats scattered micro-interactions.

**Anti-references (what it must NOT become):** generic SaaS/AI aesthetics — dark mode with glowing accents, purple→blue or cyan gradients, neon, glassmorphism everywhere, gradient text on headings/metrics, identical icon-topped card grids, everything centered, everything in a card. No bounce/elastic easing.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over spectacle.** Elevate through typography, spacing, and detail — not effects. Generous, *varied* whitespace is a feature; create rhythm, don't pad everything equally.
2. **Warm neutrals first, gold as punctuation.** Cream/ink ground carries the page; champagne gold appears sparingly for emphasis and CTAs; teal is secondary. Let photography supply the colour.
3. **Editorial hierarchy.** Oversized Playfair headlines against quiet Jakarta body; left-aligned and asymmetric compositions over centered uniformity; break the grid only for intentional emphasis.
4. **Bilingual parity (EN/AR).** Every layout must read and feel right in both LTR and RTL — mirrored spacing, alignment, and icons; Arabic typography (El Messiri) treated as a peer, never a retrofit.
5. **Accessible by default (WCAG 2.2 AA + reduced motion).** Contrast-safe pairings, always-visible focus states, comfortable tap targets, and motion that honours `prefers-reduced-motion` and degrades to legible static layouts.
